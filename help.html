Harvester Turtle

Help

Harvester Turtle keeps a simple, phone-friendly record of your positions, trades, limits, and deposits. It does not place orders or connect to your brokerage.

Quick start

  1. Use the three-line menu to add the stocks you follow.
  2. Press Log to record a buy, sale, deposit, or pending limit order.
  3. Press Refresh Prices when you want new market prices. Reloading the webpage is separate, unlimited, and does not use a price update.
  4. Use Export Data (CSV) periodically. It uses Robinhood's column layout and includes executed activity, pending LB/LS orders, and stock NOTE rows. Exporting never changes or deletes app data.
  5. Tap a stock ticker to add or read its personal note. A dot beside the ticker means a note is saved.

Trades and FIFO

For an executed trade, choose the stock, enter shares and the execution price, then confirm the calculated total.

Stock sales use FIFO: the oldest available purchase shares are sold first. Option STC and OEXP entries use the oldest matching BTO contract. A partly sold buy shows the closed portion in red with a line through it and keeps the remainder open.

For a manual option log, choose BTO, STC, or OEXP; enter the underlying stock, exact contract name, contracts, premium, and date. The app calculates contracts × premium × 100, and lets you adjust the total to include Robinhood fees.

An Opening row is the cost basis migrated from an older Harvester Turtle portfolio.

Deposits

Choose Deposit in Logs, enter the cash amount, date, and an optional note. Deposits update Total Deposited but never change stock profit/loss, FIFO, or estimated tax.

Pending limit orders

Check Limit order when the order has not filled. Pending limits are saved but excluded from shares and realized results.

Use Mark Filled when Robinhood executes it. Review the actual fill price and date before saving.

The portfolio shows compact labels such as LB-1x$130.00-5d and LS-2x$227.50-3d. LB means limit buy; LS means limit sell. The days are counted from the order date entered in the log.

Profit, tax, and Final Harvest

The app shows Stock P/L, Options P/L, and their Combined P/L separately. Option premiums and fees use the exact CSV Amount when Robinhood supplies it.

Estimated Tax = positive net realized gain × your configured rate
Final Harvest = realized profit − realized loss − estimated tax

The default tax estimate is 30%. Change it in Price & App Settings. It is a planning estimate, not tax advice.

Portfolio columns

Average Price
Open FIFO cost divided by open shares. If there are no logs, it uses the manually entered starting cost.
Invested Amount
Open cost plus money committed to pending limit buys.
Pending Limits
A read-only list of pending orders for that stock, newest order date first. Each label shows order type, shares, price with two decimals, and days since the entered order date.
High 15 / Low 15
The highest and lowest prices in the latest 15 daily market bars supplied by Twelve Data.
Goal / Harvest / Min. Cash
Your rules for when the app shows a harvest opportunity.

Harvest Price = Average Price × (1 + Growth Goal). Harvest Cash appears only after the price goal is reached and the result meets Min. Cash.

Tap or click a heading to sort the rows. On desktop, use the left/right buttons, slider, or Shift + mouse wheel to move across columns. On a phone, swipe the table sideways. Tab, Shift+Tab, and the phone keyboard's Next action move through editable fields.

Manual refresh

The number beside Refresh Prices is your configurable daily price-update limit. Reloading the webpage does not use it. A price update is counted only when at least one stock updates successfully.

If Twelve Data reports its remaining credits, the status message shows them too. On a free plan, a large portfolio may update partly; the next refresh rotates the order so the same stocks are not always skipped.

Menu options

Set Goal for All
Changes the growth goal for every tracked stock at once.
Export Data (CSV)
Downloads one Robinhood-shaped CSV containing executed Buy, Sell, BTO, STC, OEXP, and ACH deposit rows plus pending limits and stock notes. Pending buys use Trans Code LB, pending sells use LS, and stock notes use NOTE.
Import Data (CSV)
Accepts Robinhood reports and Harvester Turtle exports with the same nine columns. It imports stock activity, option BTO/STC/OEXP activity, deposits, and pending orders; keeps existing logs; adds missing stock symbols; and skips duplicates. Unsupported Robinhood activity is reported without being misclassified.
Sync Now
Checks Cloudflare for newer changes, then safely loads or saves the latest copy. Stocks, deletions, logs, and app settings sync between devices using the same private key.

Saving and safety

The app saves logs and stock notes to this browser first and can optionally sync them through your Cloudflare Worker. The CSV export contains trades, options, deposits, pending orders, and stock notes; display settings such as current price and growth goal stay in the app and cloud sync instead.

Removing a stock keeps its logs. Deleting or editing a purchase is blocked when doing so would leave a later executed sale without enough FIFO shares.
