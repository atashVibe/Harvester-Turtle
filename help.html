Harvester Turtle

Help

Harvester Turtle follows your stock positions, calculates profit, and shows when a position meets your harvest rules. It does not place trades or connect to your brokerage account.

Quick start

  1. Open the three-line menu and choose Add Stock.
  2. Enter the stock symbol when asked, then enter its buy price and the amount invested.
  3. Check the current price, growth goal, harvest percentage, and minimum cash.
  4. Read the colored lights, Action, Suggestion, and Harvest Cash results.

The table places the most-used columns first: Stock, Current Price, Buy Price, Invested, Shares, Harvest Price, Harvest Cash, and Growth Goal. Centered two-line headings, tighter spacing, and matching 11-pixel text for headings and values allow more information to fit on one screen. Stock symbols remain bold. Swipe sideways only when you need the remaining details.

The colored lights

The three signal lights appear immediately to the left of each stock symbol.

Red — Strong Buy: the Suggestion column says Strong Buy.
Yellow — Buy More: the Suggestion column says Buy More.
Green — Harvest: the Action column says Harvest.

A gray light means that signal is not active. More than one light can be active when the Action and Suggestion results are different.

Summary cards

Total Invested
The total amount you entered as invested in all stocks.
Current Value
The estimated value of all calculated shares at their current prices. It is green when it is higher than Total Invested; otherwise it is red.
Total Profit
Current Value minus Total Invested. A negative number is a loss.
Harvest Available
The total cash the rules currently allow you to harvest.

What you enter

Stock
The market symbol, such as AAPL or MRVL. You enter it when adding the row, and it is read-only afterward.
Current
The latest price received from the price service. You can also enter it manually. Money fields always use two decimal places, with the dollar sign shown in the column header.
Buy Price
Your average price paid for one share.
Invested
The total amount of money invested in that stock.
Growth Goal
The minimum profit target entered as a percentage from 0.00 to 100.00. For example, enter 3.00 for a 3% goal.
Harvest %
The part of eligible profit to harvest, entered as a percentage from 0.00 to 100.00. For example, enter 20.00 to harvest 20% of eligible profit.
Min. Cash
The smallest harvest amount you want the calculation to produce.
Previous Close
The stock's closing price from the previous regular market day.

How the harvest calculation works

Shares = Invested ÷ Buy Price
Current Value = Shares × Current Price
Profit = Current Value − Invested

The Shares column displays exactly two decimal places for readability. Calculations still use the complete share value behind the scenes.

The app calculates two profit requirements: the profit needed to meet your Growth Goal and the profit needed for your Harvest % to produce at least Min. Cash. It uses the larger requirement.

Action changes to Harvest only after the current profit reaches that larger requirement. Harvest Cash is then the current profit multiplied by the Harvest %.

Updating prices

Prices update once when the page opens. To update them again, refresh or reload the page. There is no automatic timer running in the background.

A progress bar appears immediately and advances each time an individual stock price arrives. The app requests up to eight symbols together, which is the fastest rate allowed by the free price plan.

If you have more than eight symbols, the bar shows a countdown while it waits for the next allowed group. For example, 20 symbols require three groups and take at least about two minutes on the free plan. A higher Twelve Data plan would be required for a much faster full refresh.

After a successful update, the message at the top shows Updated on: followed by the time.

The free price plan processes large portfolios in groups. Market prices may be delayed or differ from a brokerage app because data sources and trading sessions can be different.

Use Price Settings only if the Cloudflare Worker address needs to be changed.

Menu options

Add Stock
Asks for a stock symbol, then adds a new row. The symbol is fixed after the row is created.
Export
Downloads a backup of your portfolio data.
Import
Restores portfolio data from a Harvester Turtle export file.
Price Settings
Changes the Cloudflare Worker address and the private portfolio sync key used on this device.
Sync Now
Saves the current portfolio to your protected Cloudflare database immediately.

Saving and safety

When cloud sync is configured, your portfolio is stored through the protected Cloudflare Worker and can load on another device using the same private sync key. Browser storage remains available as an offline backup.

Keep the private sync key secret. Export a backup before clearing browser data or making major portfolio changes.

The remove button asks for confirmation before deleting a stock row.

Harvester Turtle is an educational calculator. Its signals are based only on the formulas shown above; they are not personalized financial advice and do not guarantee a profit.
