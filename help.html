Help

Harvester Turtle follows your stock positions, calculates profit, and shows when a position meets your harvest rules. It does not place trades or connect to your brokerage account.

Quick start

  1. Open the three-line menu and choose Add Stock.
  2. Replace NEW with the stock symbol, then enter its buy price and the amount invested.
  3. Check the current price, growth goal, harvest percentage, and minimum cash.
  4. Read the colored lights, Action, Suggestion, and Harvest Cash results.

The colored lights

Red — Strong Buy: the Suggestion column says Strong Buy.
Yellow — Buy More: the Suggestion column says Buy More.
Green — Harvest: the Action column says Harvest.

A gray light means that signal is not active. More than one light can be active when the Action and Suggestion results are different.

Summary cards

Total Invested
The total amount you entered as invested in all stocks.
Current Value
The estimated value of all calculated shares at their current prices. It is green when it is higher than Total Invested; otherwise it is red.
Total Profit
Current Value minus Total Invested. A negative number is a loss.
Harvest Available
The total cash the rules currently allow you to harvest.

What you enter

Stock
The market symbol, such as AAPL or MRVL.
Current
The latest price received from the price service. You can also enter it manually.
Buy Price
Your average price paid for one share.
Invested
The total amount of money invested in that stock.
Growth Goal
The minimum profit target written as a decimal. For example, 0.03 means 3%.
Harvest %
The part of eligible profit to harvest. For example, 0.20 means 20%.
Min. Cash
The smallest harvest amount you want the calculation to produce.
Previous Close
The stock's closing price from the previous regular market day.

How the harvest calculation works

Shares = Invested ÷ Buy Price
Current Value = Shares × Current Price
Profit = Current Value − Invested

The app calculates two profit requirements: the profit needed to meet your Growth Goal and the profit needed for your Harvest % to produce at least Min. Cash. It uses the larger requirement.

Action changes to Harvest only after the current profit reaches that larger requirement. Harvest Cash is then the current profit multiplied by the Harvest %.

Updating prices

Prices update once when the page opens. To update them again, refresh or reload the page. There is no automatic timer running in the background.

After a successful update, the message at the top shows Updated on: followed by the time.

The free price plan processes large portfolios in groups. Market prices may be delayed or differ from a brokerage app because data sources and trading sessions can be different.

Use Price Settings only if the Cloudflare Worker address needs to be changed.

Menu options

Add Stock
Adds a new editable row.
Export
Downloads a backup of your portfolio data.
Import
Restores portfolio data from a Harvester Turtle export file.
Price Settings
Changes the price-service address used when the page is refreshed.

Saving and safety

Your portfolio entries are saved in the current browser on the current device. Export a backup before clearing browser data or moving to another device, then use Import on the new device.

Harvester Turtle is an educational calculator. Its signals are based only on the formulas shown above; they are not personalized financial advice and do not guarantee a profit.
