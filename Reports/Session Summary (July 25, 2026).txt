Current calculation rules:
- Shares = Invested ÷ Buy Price
- Current Value = Shares × Current Price
- Profit = Current Value − Invested
- Goal Profit = Invested × Growth Goal
- Minimum Required Profit = Minimum Cash ÷ Harvest %
- Required Profit = the larger of Goal Profit and Minimum Required Profit
- Harvest Price = (Invested + Required Profit) ÷ Shares
- Harvest Cash = Profit × Harvest %, but only after Required Profit is reached
